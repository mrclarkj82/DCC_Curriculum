# Slide Brief: Titles, Transitions, and Simple Motion

Create a 9-slide classroom deck for a 90-minute high school Digital Content Creators block.

## Slide Sequence

1. Title, learning target, and today's evidence goal.
2. Hook: When does a title help a video, and when can it distract from the video?
3. Why this matters for video production students.
4. Vocabulary mini-map: Effects panel, Transition, Title, Lower third, Generator.
5. Interface focus: where this workflow happens in DaVinci Resolve.
6. Teacher demo steps: Open the edited sequence from earlier lessons. / Add one readable title card or lower third using text controls. / Adjust font size, placement, duration, and contrast so the title is readable..
7. Common mistakes and fixes.
8. Independent work checklist and evidence expectations.
9. Reflection and exit ticket: What title, transition, or motion choice did you add, and why does it belong in the edit?

## Visual Suggestions

- Use dark synthwave backgrounds with bright cyan, magenta, and orange labels.
- Include Resolve interface screenshots or placeholders with clear arrows.
- Use short labels and large readable UI callouts instead of paragraphs.
- Show before/after or messy/organized comparisons where possible.

## Speaker Notes

Keep the deck focused on one practical workflow. Students should spend most of the period working in Resolve, not copying notes.

### Pause-and-Practice Prompts

- After adding text: read the title at normal viewer size and check its spelling.
- After adjusting the Inspector: play the title for its full duration and check its position and contrast.
- After the motion demonstration: save a readable static title first; attempt keyframes only after the core work is complete.

### Current Required Student Workflow

1. Open the cleaned Foundations_MiniEdit timeline and save.
2. Watch 01:20:08-01:30:41. Pause after the Effects panel, basic text/title controls, and Inspector/keyframe demonstrations, and try the matching action in your project.

### Reflection and Differentiation

Reflection: In 2-3 sentences, explain what your title tells the viewer, how you made it readable, and why you chose a transition or a clean cut.

Intervention: Use a basic static text title, a teacher-approved font, and one safe placement example. Assess spelling, readability, and timing before optional animation.

Extension: Animate a title position with two keyframes or write a reusable lower-third style guide; keep the motion readable.

Teacher check: A static title and clean cuts can meet the core requirement. Basic title work stays in the Edit page; a Fusion chapter or Studio-only effect is not required.
