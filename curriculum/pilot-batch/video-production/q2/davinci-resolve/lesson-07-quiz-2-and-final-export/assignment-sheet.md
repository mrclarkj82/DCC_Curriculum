# Final Resolve Mini-Edit Export Assignment Sheet

## Objective

I can complete a final checkpoint and export a small edited video with clear naming, useful render settings, and a reflection on my post-production workflow.

## Watch, Pause, Practice

Open the assigned tutorial segment and your own DaVinci Resolve project together. Watch a short demonstration, pause, repeat the action in your project, and check the result before continuing. Keep using the same saved project across all seven lessons. Watching the video alone does not complete the assignment. Assigned segment: 04:53:29-05:06:57.

- After render settings: check output folder, file name, single-clip export, frame rate, MP4 format, and H.264 codec.
- After adding to the queue: check the queued job before starting the render.
- After rendering: open the exported movie outside Resolve and watch it from beginning to end before submitting.

## Required Steps

1. Complete the teacher-assigned Quiz 2 checkpoint for titles, transitions, and export readiness. If no quiz is assigned, show your readable title and explain your intended export format to your teacher for a checkoff.
2. Open your polished 30-60 second mini-edit. Jump directly to 04:53:29 and stop at 05:06:57; do not work through the intervening Cut, Fusion, Color, or Fairlight chapters.
3. Play the full edit and check its beginning/middle/end, title readability, basic Edit-page audio, and absence of accidental gaps.
4. On the Deliver page, follow the render-settings and queue demonstrations. Make a single-clip MP4/H.264 review export using the project's frame rate and a resolution supported by your source footage and classroom computer.
5. Save the movie as LastName_FirstName_VP-Q2-A07_Final in your approved Exports folder, add it to the render queue, render, and play the exported file outside Resolve to confirm that picture and sound work.
6. Place the exported video in Google Drive or an approved YouTube location and submit its share link in DCC. Also submit an evidence Doc/Drive link with your Deliver/render-queue screenshot and quiz result or dated teacher checkoff; complete the reflection and exit ticket.

## Naming Convention

Project: LastName_FirstName_ResolveFoundations. Timeline: Foundations_MiniEdit. Evidence files: LastName_FirstName_VP-Q2-A07_Description. Keep original source media in the approved folder. Save the project at every checkpoint.

## Evidence and Submission

For Lessons 1-6, collect the required screenshots and short notes in one Google Doc or approved Drive folder, paste the share link into DCC, complete the evidence checklist, and type the reflection. An exported video is not required for these checkpoints. Lesson 7 requires a final exported video link plus an export-settings screenshot. Save project work locally; DCC collects evidence links, not raw project or media uploads.

- A Google Drive or approved YouTube share link to your playable 30-60 second final mini-edit with at least four clips and a readable title/lower third.
- A Google Docs or Drive link containing a screenshot of the Deliver settings or completed render queue and the teacher-assigned Quiz 2 result or a dated teacher checkpoint/checkoff note.
- A 2-3 sentence reflection entered in DCC explaining one editing decision, one export check, and one skill you will use in the next video project.

## Reflection Prompt

In 2-3 sentences, explain one editing decision, describe how you verified the exported movie, and name one skill you will use in the next video project.

## What To Do If Stuck

Use a teacher-approved MP4/H.264 preset and a 30-second four-clip edit. If rendering fails, capture the error and settings for teacher review; mark the final video as needing revision rather than substituting a timeline screenshot for a successful export.

## 4-Point Rubric

- 4: All required Resolve actions and evidence are complete, and the reflection explains a thoughtful improvement. The submitted 30-60 second movie plays outside Resolve and includes four clips, a readable title, basic audio, and verified export settings.
- 3: Required Resolve actions, evidence, and reflection are complete and meet the stated lesson checklist.
- 2: Work is partly complete, or a required screenshot, note, playback check, or reflection is missing or unclear.
- 1: Some work was attempted, but the evidence does not yet demonstrate the required Resolve workflow.
- 0: No assessable evidence submitted.

Compare a smaller review copy with a higher-quality archive export and explain when each is useful.
