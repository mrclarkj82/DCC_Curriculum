## Vocabulary

- Merge: A node that combines a foreground image with a background image.
- Foreground/background: The image placed over another image and the image underneath it.
- Mask: A shape or image that limits where a tool affects the result.
- Polygon: A mask made from connected points and curve handles.
- Fast Noise: A generated texture that can provide smoke or fog patterns.
- Soft edge: A gradual mask boundary that helps an effect blend into the shot.
