## Vocabulary and Misconceptions

- Composite: An image built by combining two or more visual elements.
- Plate: A source shot used as one layer of a visual-effects composite.
- Transform: A node that changes an image's position, size, or rotation.
- Invert: Reversing which part of a mask is included or excluded.
- Tracker: A tool that follows a visible feature over time to drive motion.
- Match move: Using tracked movement to attach a foreground element to a moving shot.

Do not equate watching with doing. Explain that different page views share timeline work, nodes must connect to the final output, and paid tools are optional.

Intervention: Use a simple static two-element composite with a broad mask and a short, clearly moving shot. Use manual position keyframes if tracking is unreliable. Reduce the clip duration before adding complex lighting or paid features.

Extension: Add a reusable tracked mask or compare corrections placed before and after Merge. Explain why the selected node order is appropriate. Studio-only demonstrations remain optional.
