## Objective

## Required Steps

1. Continue in Foundations_FusionPractice. Use the supplied alien/ship pair and moving wide shot, or teacher-approved foreground/background and moving footage. Keep the original sources available.
2. Watch 02:27:02-02:50:28 in short portions. Pause at the two-image setup, polygon/invert and Transform, then the moving-graphic demonstration. The Magic Mask demonstration is viewing-only; do not buy Studio or require that tool.
3. Build a two-element composite with a Merge and a polygon mask. For the ship example, invert the cockpit mask to reveal the alien. With substitute footage, explain which area the mask reveals and why.
4. Use a Transform to fit the inserted element. Check its scale, position, mask edge, and node order at the beginning and end. Keep additional lighting/color branches optional.
5. On a short moving shot, add a simple visible text label or frame. Make it follow a subject using the regular Tracker/Match Move demonstration or manual position keyframes. Check at least the beginning, middle, and end and correct visible drift.
6. Save. Submit the composite/node-flow screenshot, three frame captures showing the moving graphic, and notes about your Transform, mask, and motion method. Submit the evidence link and DCC reflection; no export is required today.

## What To Do If Stuck

Use a simple static two-element composite with a broad mask and a short, clearly moving shot. Use manual position keyframes if tracking is unreliable. Reduce the clip duration before adding complex lighting or paid features.
